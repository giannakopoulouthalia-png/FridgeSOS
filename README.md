🧊 FridgeSOS

Save food. Save money. Survive student life.

FridgeSOS is a student-friendly food management application designed to help university students reduce food waste, discover affordable recipes, and organize their cooking around their busy schedules.

✨ Features

🥬 My Fridge — Add, edit, and organize ingredients while tracking expiration dates.

🚨 Fridge Emergency — Identify ingredients that need to be used soon.

🍳 Recipe Recommender — Explore personalized recipe suggestions based on available ingredients, preferences, and budget.

🔖 Saved Recipes — Bookmark your favorite recipes for easy access.

📅 Smart Student Planner — Import a university timetable or enter classes manually to discover possible cooking times.

💸 Weekly Budget Predictor (Planned) — Estimate upcoming cooking expenses based on the weekly schedule, available ingredients, and suggested meals.

👤 Personal Profile — Customize dietary preferences, allergies, cooking time, and skill level.
